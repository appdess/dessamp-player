# DessAmp community code of conduct

## Our commitment

DessAmp aims to be a technically rigorous, welcoming project. Maintainers and
contributors are expected to treat each other with respect, assume good faith,
accept constructive criticism, and make room for people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, personal attacks, and publication of
another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Where it applies

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
documentation, project-controlled communication channels, and situations in
which someone is representing DessAmp publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not publish sensitive incident details in an issue. Before the repository is
public, report conduct concerns to the maintainer through an established private
channel. Once private repository reporting is enabled, use that private
reporting route unless a dedicated conduct address is published.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy as far as
practical, hear relevant context, and apply a proportionate response. Responses
may include a correction request, warning, temporary participation restriction,
or permanent removal from project spaces. Retaliation against a reporter or
participant in an investigation is itself a violation.
